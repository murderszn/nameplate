One system, two tools

Nameplate is built as a pair: a fast, offline-first tool for the person standing in the unit, and a console for the person running the portfolio.

For technicians

Nameplate Field

Scan the plate. See the history. Log the fix.

  • Sub-3-second QR scan, fully offline
  • Claim and tag a new asset in under 15 seconds
  • Full service history, parts lineage, and photos on every asset
  • Turn walkthroughs that generate work orders automatically

What you get on day one

No modules to turn on later. The registry is the product, and everything below runs on top of it.

Asset registry

Every washer, dryer, range, HVAC unit and water heater — tagged, dated, and located, down to the unit.

Service history

One reverse-chronological timeline per asset: every service event, move, and part swap, with who and when.

Work orders

Assigned, prioritized, tracked against SLA, and closed with the service event attached — not a separate system to reconcile.

Shrinkage detection

Assets not confirmed in N days surface on a review list, without ever pointing a finger at a person.

Cost tracking

Cost captured on every service event, even estimated, so repair-vs-replace is decided on your own numbers.

Turn walkthroughs

A checklist generated from the unit's actual asset roster. Complete it once; work orders write themselves.

The plate is the record

Every major appliance already ships with a nameplate — the stamped plate carrying manufacturer, model, serial, and date code. Ours is the one that doesn't fade, doesn't get painted over, and turns into a permanent digital record the moment it's scanned.
