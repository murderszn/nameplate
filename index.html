03 / THE CORE LEAK

Where multifamily capital quietly dissolves.

The industry runs high-value physical equipment on memory, group chats, and corroded manufacturer serial plates that no technician can read.

01

Silent Turnover Shrinkage

Equipment moves during make-ready turns or functional components get swapped without records. Without a physical chain of custody, the loss is quietly absorbed as operating friction.

02

Uncaptured Warranty Coverage

Technicians replace failed components on active equipment with zero awareness that the part is covered under manufacturer warranty. Portfolios pay out of pocket for repairs that are already guaranteed.

03

Blind Capital Allocation

Asset managers discover aging equipment clusters only when failures cause emergency tenant disruption. CapEx is forced into reactive crisis management instead of planned lifecycle replacement.
