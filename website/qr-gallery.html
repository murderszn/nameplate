02 / ANATOMY OF A NAMEPLATE TAG

Every mark has a job.

Click through the construction of the plate. The visual language is deliberately industrial: legible at a glance, expressive up close.

A / 01

Finder patterns

Three orientation anchors let a native camera understand rotation, distance, and surface angle before it reads the payload.

A / 02

NPID identity

A compact, human-readable identifier gives the field team something to call out over radio, even without a scan.

A / 03

Security layer

Reflective texture and VOID watermarking make removal visible. The label is evidence, not decoration.

A / 04

Telemetry cue

The instruction is direct: scan with a native camera and resolve the record. No app-training ceremony required.

A / 05

Material spec

3M-7881 tamper-destructible vinyl signals the physical contract: waterproof, permanent, and meant to fragment when peeled.

A / 06

ECC capacity

High error correction keeps the code recoverable after scratches, grime, glare, and the ordinary violence of a utility closet.

A / 07

Corner registration

Small red marks create a print and inspection reference, giving production and audit teams a common visual vocabulary.

A / 08

IP67 promise

The top line compresses the environmental expectation: dust-tight, water-resistant, and ready for real field conditions.
